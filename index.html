EXPLORE THE WONDERS OF
SANTA ROSA, LAGUNA

Santa Rosa is a city in Laguna, a light industrial and commercial hub close to Metro Manila. Major tourist draws are the Enchanted Kingdom theme park, the Nuvali mixed-use development, and shopping spots like SM City Santa Rosa and Paseo de Santa Rosa. Santa Rosa is a major stopover for travelers to Tagaytay via Santa Rosa-Tagaytay Road (Route 420).

ABOUT THE DESTINATION

Santa Rosa is a city located in the province of Laguna, Philippines, approximately 38 kilometers south of Manila. Known as the “Gateway to the South,” it has transformed from a predominantly agricultural area into one of the most rapidly urbanizing cities in the region. The city is part of the Calabarzon region and serves as an important economic hub, attracting both residential and commercial developments. It has a rich history, initially established as a town during the Spanish colonial period in 1578, and it was eventually converted into a city in 1969. The city is named after Saint Rose of Lima, the first canonized saint of the Americas.
